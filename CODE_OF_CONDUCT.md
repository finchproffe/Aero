# Community conduct

Aero welcomes respectful technical disagreement.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, and doxxing;
- respect privacy and never share another person's browsing data;
- disclose conflicts of interest when proposing advertising, search, analytics, or commercial integrations;
- follow project security-reporting rules.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove community content that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.
